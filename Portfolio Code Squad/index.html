About Me
My name is Ismail, and I’m originally from Harar, Ethiopia. I moved to the United States after high school and became interested in technology through my curiosity about computers and coding. Today, I’m studying Computer Science and building my skills in web development. My goal is to become a software engineer and eventually explore AI and machine learning. Being part of CodeSquad is a great opportunity for me to further my knowledge and skills in technology. I’m excited to learn more, work on projects, and build a stronger foundation as I continue working toward my goals in tech.
CodeSquad Mini Course Completed

CSS- I learned the basics of CSS, including how to style elements, change colors, adjust spacing, and use different selectors. The Cat Photo App project was really helpful because it let me practice CSS by building and styling a real webpage instead of only learning the concepts.

Html- This was one of the easiest assignments for me to catch on to quickly. I learned how to structure a webpage using HTML, including headings, paragraphs, links, images, lists, and forms. The Cat Photo App helped me understand how different HTML elements work together to build a complete webpage.

Computer Basic- This was the most challenging assignment for me because there was a lot of new information to learn in a short amount of time. I learned some of the basics, but most of my understanding is still at a surface level. I know I need to practice JavaScript more, and I am sure I will master it with the help and support of CodeSquad.
